# Family Calendar — No-Upfront-Cost Plan

## What can be done without paying first
- The app source can be developed and prepared locally.
- A release APK can be generated on a machine with Android Studio/SDK.
- The app can be distributed privately for free during validation.
- Firebase has free tiers suitable for early development, subject to current quotas.
- AdMob does not require an upfront registration payment; actual ad serving and monetization require a valid AdMob account and policy compliance.

## What cannot be bypassed
Google Play requires a one-time US$25 developer registration fee for a Play Console developer account. There is no legitimate way to publish a normal public app on Google Play without a registered developer account.

For new personal developer accounts created after 13 November 2023, Google also requires a closed test with at least 12 testers opted in continuously for 14 days before production access.

## Recommended path if the budget is zero today
1. Finish the Family Calendar release candidate.
2. Generate and privately distribute the APK.
3. Collect early users and feedback.
4. Build the first audience and validate retention.
5. When the $25 is available, register Play Console.
6. Complete the required closed test and publish.
